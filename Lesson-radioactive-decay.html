Radioactive decay

Learning objective: How are atomic and mass numbers affected by different kinds of decay?

What is an alpha particle?

helium nucleus or two protons and two neutrons

What is a beta particle?

electron ejected from the nucleus of an atom

What is the relative mass and relative charge of an alpha particle?

4 and +2

What happens to the nucleus of an atom when it emits an alpha particle?

becomes more stable/ changes to a different element
