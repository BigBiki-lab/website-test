What is an induced magnet?

something that is a magnet only because it is in the magnetic field of another magnet

Name two materials that an induced magnet could be made from.

iron, cobalt, nickel, steel

A moving charge creates a magnetic field

When a current flows through a long, straight conductor (e.g. a wire) a magnetic field is created around it.
The field is made up of concentric circles perpendicular to the wire, with the wire in the centre.

The right-hand thumb rule

Using your right hand, point your thumb in the direction of current and curl your fingers.The direction of your fingers is the direction of the field.

How can you change the direction of the magnetic field around a wire?

Changing the direction of the current changes the direction of the magnetic field - use the right-hand thumb rule to work out which way it goes.

Solenoid

A coil of wire with electricity flowing in it. Also called an electromagnet.

How can you change the direction and strength of the magnetic field around a solenoid?

To change the direction of the magnetic field: Reverse the direction of the electric current through the solenoid.
To change the strength of the magnetic field: Increase or decrease the current, increase the number of turns of the coil, or insert a soft iron core into the solenoid.

Solenoid - electromagnet

The fields from individual coils add together to form a very strong field inside the solenoid.
Outside the solenoid the fields from one side of the coil tend to cancel out the fields from the other side to give a weaker field outside.

Stronger magnetic field

The magnetic field of an electromagnet/solenoid can be made stronger by putting a piece of iron (an iron core) inside the coil.
This iron becomes a temporary magnet/induced magnet - it is only magnetic while the field from the electromagnet is affecting it.
